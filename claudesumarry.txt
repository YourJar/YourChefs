The project
An indoor navigation app for VIT Vellore, aimed at first-years and second-years who get lost inside confusing campus buildings. Long-term vision includes a Street View-style "freestyle roam" mode and a "directional" guided mode — but for the hackathon, we're building a scoped-down, working MVP first.
